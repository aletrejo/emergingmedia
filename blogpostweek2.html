Week 2 Responses

This week’s readings tied nicely into some of the questions I had after reading Hito Steyerl’s In Defense of the Poor Image. Specifically, I was wondering if AI-generated images could be considered poor images. After all, they were made by the masses for the masses and had a similar life trajectory as the poor images that Steyerl described where AI images are primarily meant for quick distribution with poor regard to quality or meaning. Having read both The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction and AI’s AURA:Aesthetic, Ubiquitous, Regimented Automation, now I’m inclined to say that AI generated images aren’t poor images because the technology behind these images differ from the poor images in their fascist nature.
